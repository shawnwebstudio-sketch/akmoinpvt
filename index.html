Questions

Asked on every site visit.

Is the site visit really free?

Yes. We measure, suggest material and give a written quote at no charge within city limits. Outside the city a small travel fee is adjusted in the final bill.

Do you supply material or only labour?

Both. Every catalog item can be labour-only or labour plus material. Material bills are handed to you so you know the brands used.

How is painting area calculated?

For interiors we take carpet area × 3.5 to cover walls and ceiling, then measure exactly on site. Doors and windows are deducted.

How do payments work?

Usually 30% to start, 40% at mid-stage and 30% after handover. UPI, bank transfer and cash accepted.

PVC panels or epoxy floor: which suits my home?

PVC fluted and UV-marble panels dress up walls in a day with no paint or polish, and they never get damp. 3D metallic epoxy gives a seamless, mirror-glossy floor that is easy to mop. We bring samples of both on the site visit.
